P3 Paper Plastic Products – Android app

Your reorder list, your own prices and ordering, in one tap.

Download the app (Android)

  1. Tap Download the app, then open the downloaded P3Store.apk.
  2. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or WhatsApp, go back and tap Install.
  3. Open P3 Store and sign in with Google or your email – you only do this once.
  4. WhatsApp reorder links from P3 now open straight in the app.

iPhone: please use the link in our WhatsApp message – it opens your reorder list in Safari.
